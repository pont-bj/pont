#### Available No-Redirect Payment Methods

In this mode, users remain on the website or application to complete their payment without being redirected to another payment interface. This mode is ideal for a smooth and fast user flow.

> **BENIN**
>
> `MTN` (mtn\_open),
> `Moov` (moov),
> `Celtiis` (sbin)

> **TOGO**
>
> `Moov` (moov\_tg),
> `TogoCom` (togocel)

> **GUINEA**
>
> `MTN Guinea` (mtn\_open\_gn)

> **CÔTE D'IVOIRE**
>
> `MTN` (mtn\_ci)

> **NIGER**
>
> `Airtel Niger` (airtel\_ne)

> **SENEGAL**
>
> `Free Senegal` (free\_sn)
